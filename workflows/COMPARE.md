# Compare the two tracks

Checked on 3 October 2026. Vendors change limits without notice, so follow the
links to re-check before you rely on a number. "Not published" means the
vendor says a limit exists but gives no figure.

## At a glance

| | Personal track | Corporate track |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | Newest features, including video and image generation | Cleared for work information; sits where your documents already live |
| Limitation | Public, non-sensitive content only | Fewer media features; no generated video found |
| Cost | Free tiers exist; paid plans remove most waiting | No cost to the user; licence type decides what you get |
| Limits | Published for some tools, vague for others | Almost nothing published; ask internally |
| Main caveat | You are responsible for what you paste in | What you can do depends on your licence and local settings |

## The six gates you will meet

A "gate" is the point where a tool makes you wait, pay or accept less.

| Gate | Plain meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Rolling window | Your allowance refills some hours after you use it | Gemini refreshes every 5 hours |
| Daily cap | A fixed allowance each day | Google Flow: 50 credits a day |
| Weekly cap | A second, longer ceiling | Gemini and Claude both apply one |
| Monthly pool | A bucket of credits or uses that refills monthly | Canva Free: up to 20 AI uses a month |
| Capacity throttling | No fixed number; busy times give you less | Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat "standard access" |
| Model downgrade | You carry on, but on a smaller model | Gemini continues on a lighter model |

The cooling-off period is simply how long the gate stays shut: 5 hours, a day,
a week or until your billing date.

## You will not see "tokens"

Tokens are fragments of text that the model reads and writes. They drive the
cost behind the scenes, but none of these apps shows you a token count. Count
what the app shows instead:

| Tool | What to count |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Limit notices per feature (images, uploads, voice) |
| Gemini | Percentage of your 5-hour and weekly limit, in Settings |
| Google Flow | Credits per video |
| Claude | Share of your 5-hour session and weekly limit |
| Canva | "Uses" from your monthly allowance |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat | A notice when capacity is unavailable |
| Pair, Agentsea | Nothing published |

| Tool | Free tier | Paid adds | Re-check at |

Check in the app.

## Corporate tools

| Tool | What is publicly documented | Not published |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat (no paid licence) | Standard access that varies with capacity; file upload; image generation | Image and upload counts |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot (paid licence) | Priority access; Copilot inside Word, Excel and PowerPoint; answers grounded in your organisation's files | Counts |
| Pair | Free to public officers; cleared for data up to Restricted / Sensitive Normal; build and share assistants; document upload | Usage limits; whether patient data is permitted |
| Agentsea | Build AI agents in plain language; approved tools and connectors; audit logs and monitoring. In-app guidance: documents up to Restricted / Sensitive Normal; Reference Files up to 10 files of 30 MB each; Knowledge Spaces; Browse SharePoint | Models, quotas |
