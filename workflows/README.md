## The pattern behind every workflow

1. **Define done first.** Audience, purpose, format, length, deadline.
2. **Supply the source.** Give the assistant your real content.
3. **Plan before producing.** Outline, storyboard or content plan first.
4. **Produce.**
5. **Check.** You remain accountable for what goes out under your name.

## House rules

- **No health information** on the personal track, ever. On the corporate track,
  only where your institution's policy explicitly allows it.
- **Source over invention.** Verify any fact, figure or reference the
  assistant adds, or delete it.
- **No real people's likeness** in generated images or video.
- **Say when AI was used.** Label AI-generated images and video.
- **Get the usual approvals.** AI does not replace communications, ethics
  board or supervisor sign-off.
