# Personal track: cost cards

For your own device and your own AI accounts. Use public, de-identified,
non-sensitive content only. No health information. No internal documents.

Checked on 3 October 2026. Times are estimates until measured. See
[COMPARE.md](COMPARE.md) for what the gates mean.

## 1. Storyboard to video

| | Free | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Tools | Any chat assistant for the script; Google Flow for clips; any video editor | Same, with a Google AI plan |
| Working time | 60 to 90 minutes | 60 to 90 minutes |
| Usage consumed | 20 credits per fast clip | 20 credits per fast clip |
| Gate | 50 credits a day, so 2 fast clips a day | Monthly pool, 1,000 on Google AI Pro |
| Elapsed time for ten clips | About 5 days, more with retakes. About 2 days on the lite model | Same day |
| Can it finish? | Yes, slowly | Yes |
| Caveat | Peak-hour blocking; lower resolution | Check how many videos one request makes before you submit |

The arithmetic: 50 credits divided by 20 is 2 clips a day; ten clips divided
by 2 is 5 days. This is why video is the clearest example of a time gate.
