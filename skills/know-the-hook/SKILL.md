# Know the hook, keep the engagement

## How to run this skill

1. The prompt below has gaps in square brackets. Fill any gap the
   conversation already answers. Ask the person for the rest in one
   message, then wait for their answers.
2. Follow the prompt as if they had pasted it, with their answers in place.
3. Where the prompt says the person decides, stop and let them decide.

## The prompt

I am planning [a session, lesson or talk] for [audience, group size, what they already know]. It lasts [how long] and runs [in person or online].

Suggest three ways to open it. Each must be something the audience does, not something it watches. Each should take under [number] minutes and need only what people already have in their hands, such as a phone. Everyone should be able to start without help, then be stretched a little.

Then suggest one moment every [10] minutes that brings the audience's own answers back into view.

For each suggestion, tell me in a few lines what the audience does, what I show, what could go wrong and the fallback if the technology fails.

Keep answers anonymous: totals only, nothing that identifies anyone. Flag anything that needs the internet or an outside service, and tell me what to check beforehand.

If you need to know more about my audience, ask me first. I know the room, so I will choose.
