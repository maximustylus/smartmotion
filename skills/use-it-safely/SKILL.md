# Use it safely

## How to run this skill

1. The prompt below has gaps in square brackets. Fill any gap the
   conversation already answers. Ask the person for the rest in one
   message, then wait for their answers.
2. Follow the prompt as if they had pasted it, with their answers in place.
3. Where the prompt says the person decides, stop and let them decide.

## The prompt

I am about to [send, post or present] the material below. Be my second pair of eyes. Do not fix anything yet. Report first, and keep each answer short.

1. Privacy: does it contain any health information, colleague details or internal documents? If yes, stop and tell me what to remove.
2. Facts: list each factual claim. Mark it unverified unless the material names its source. Never supply a source from memory.
3. Images and clips: for each, note where the material says it came from, or mark it unknown. Do not judge real or fake by looks. Flag any I may need permission to use.
4. People: flag anything said about a named person that goes beyond what the source says.
5. Misreading: what is the worst way this could be misread, and what one line would prevent it?

If you cannot tell, say so. End with what I still need to check myself.

The material: [paste it with health and personal details removed, or describe it]
