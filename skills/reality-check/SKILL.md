## How to run this skill

1. The prompt below has gaps in square brackets. Fill any gap the
   conversation already answers. Ask the person for the rest in one
   message, then wait for their answers.
2. Follow the prompt as if they had pasted it, with their answers in place.
3. Where the prompt says the person decides, stop and let them decide.

## The prompt

I have a working demo. Show me what is left to do.

What works today: [describe the demo]
Who will use it, and where: [people, devices, setting]

List everything between this demo and something I could hand to a stranger. Group it under six headings:
1. Correctness: is the result right every time?
2. When things go wrong: an accidental tap, a lost connection.
3. Data and privacy: what is stored, and who can see it?
4. Accessibility: can everyone read and use it?
5. Speed: is it quick on an old phone?
6. Upkeep: who fixes and updates it later?

For each heading, say roughly how much work is left compared with what is done so far, and name the one item most likely to take longest. Say where you are guessing.

Be blunt, in plain words. If the honest answer is that the final 10% is most of the work, say that first. Do not fix anything yet. I will decide what is worth finishing.
