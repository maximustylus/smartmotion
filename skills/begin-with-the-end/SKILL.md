# Begin with the end in mind

## How to run this skill

1. The prompt below has gaps in square brackets. Fill any gap the
   conversation already answers. Ask the person for the rest in one
   message, then wait for their answers.
2. Follow the prompt as if they had pasted it, with their answers in place.
3. Where the prompt says the person decides, stop and let them decide.

## The prompt

I want to build [what, in one line: a session, a handout, an app].

Before you build anything, help me write a one-page brief with five headings:

1. Purpose: who it is for, and what changes for them once it exists.
2. Done means: three to five yes-or-no checks that must all be true.
3. Out of scope: what we will not build this time.
4. Limits: where it will be used, the time I have, information to keep out of it (for example, health details), and anything that must never happen.
5. Phases: the order of work, with a stop for my approval after each.

Ask only what you need to know, one question at a time, in plain language. Wait for each reply. If I am unsure, offer two or three options.

Then show me the whole brief. Keep every line short. Mark anything you assume with the word TODO, so I can confirm it. Do not start building until I say the brief is right.
