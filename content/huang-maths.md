# Jensen Huang on basic maths: what he said

The owner's own check, "Jensen Huang on Basic Maths: Key Findings" (Muhammad
Alif Bin Abu Bakar, 7 October 2026), with each quote checked again against
the news reports on 7 October 2026. The original podcast episode was not
heard; every quote below is as reported.

## Bottom line

Nvidia's Chief Executive Officer, Jensen Huang, said on The Ezra Klein Show
(The New York Times, released 23 September 2026) that forgetting basic maths
because of AI probably does not matter. He did not say "kids don't need maths
skills": that is a Medium headline, and it overstates his words.

## Context and caveats

- What prompted it: Klein cited a study of about 26,000 students in China
  who began using AI at different times. Homework got faster; later exam
  scores and retention fell (Futurism: the skills "melted away, resulting in
  lower exam scores in the long-term").
- No report names the study's authors or journal, so it is unverified.
  Present it only as "a study Klein cited".
- The Medium piece is member-only opinion, read only in part.

## Takeaways for educators (the owner's)

1. Faster completion is not the same as learning: check what learners can
   do without AI.
2. Decide which foundational skills your learners must keep, rather than
   letting the tool decide.
3. Quote Huang's own words, not the headline.
