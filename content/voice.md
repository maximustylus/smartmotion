# Voice guide

Agreed in the content pass of 5 October 2026 and followed by every line of
copy in the app. Written by the content team for the owner to adjust.

THROUGH-LINE. A stroll from wonder to judgement: the more the assistant can do, the more your judgement matters, and each move is one deliberate act that keeps the two in balance and you in charge.

USE. Assistant, never bot, model or "the AI"; a source's "chatbot" stays. Agent: an assistant that takes several steps on its own. Prompt: your words to the assistant; under a move, never "cheatsheet". Check: what you do with every answer. "You" is the reader; "we" is shared history; "I" is Alif, rarely. One analogy: a capable junior. You brief them, check and countersign their work; you stay accountable.

AVOID. Ship, deploy, repo, tokens, guardrails, vibecoded, unless the line explains them. Gloss vibe coding: building by describing, without reading the code. Simply, obviously, "just" as a minimiser. Two walking words in one move. Pointing to another scene: each stands alone, abbreviations spelt out, except AI.

RHYTHM. Name: an instruction. Angle: one plain picture, one breath. Principle: two or three short sentences. End on your part: the assistant proposes, you decide. A shape, not a slogan. See why: who said it, what they claimed, how we use it; never "proven". See how: one real case and what it shows an educator. Try it: one line beginning "Paste this", naming the moment.

BOTH READERS. Plain word first, exact term second, once: "made-up answers, called hallucinations". Plain is not vague: names, numbers and sources stay exact. Not "garbage in, garbage out" but "Vague brief, confident guess". If an expert winces or a newcomer stalls, rewrite.
