## A. Tokens versus people

1. For narrow text tasks, AI computing cost was about thirty times cheaper
   than crowd workers per item (2023 prices).
   Source: Gilardi, Alizadeh and Kubli (2023), Proceedings of the National
   Academy of Sciences, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2305016120
2. The price of a fixed level of AI capability has fallen by a median of about
   50 times a year.
   Source: Cottier, Snodin, Owen and Adamczewski (2025), Epoch AI,
   https://epoch.ai/data-insights/llm-inference-price-trends
3. In a randomised trial, AI scribes cut note-writing time by about 0.36 hours
   per practitioner per day and reduced exhaustion. No staff reductions were
   reported.
   Source: Afshar et al. (2025), NEJM AI,
   https://doi.org/10.1056/AIoa2500945
4. When expert review is included, AI's advantage on professional tasks
   shrinks from about 100 times to under 2 times.
   Source: OpenAI (2025), GDPval, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04374
   Caveat: vendor source, and the review-adjusted figure was read from a
   secondary summary. Confirm in the paper's appendix or drop it.
5. Experienced developers believed AI made them 20% faster. Measured, they
   were 19% slower (early-2025 tools).
   Source: Becker, Rush, Barnes and Rein (2025), METR,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089

Message for the slide: cost is total cost of ownership (licences, set-up,
checking, correcting, governance) against time genuinely freed, not the token
bill.

Do NOT claim: that an AI tool equals a fraction of a staff member; that AI is
100 times cheaper than experts; any time or money saved by Note Buddy.

## B. Gates

6. ChatGPT Free has unlimited everyday text chats; images, uploads, voice and
   data analysis are still limited.
   Source: OpenAI Help Center,
   https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9275245-chatgpt-free-tier-faq
7. Gemini's limits refresh every 5 hours, up to a weekly limit.
   Source: Google, https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en
8. Google Flow gives every user 50 credits a day; one fast clip costs 20.
   Source: Google, https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234?hl=en
9. Pair is cleared for data up to Restricted / Sensitive Normal; consumer
   tools are for public data only.
   Source: GovTech Developer Portal,
   https://www.developer.tech.gov.sg/products/categories/productivity-tools/pair/overview

All figures checked 3 October 2026 and liable to change. Show the date.
