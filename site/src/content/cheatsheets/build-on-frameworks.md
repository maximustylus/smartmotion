# Build on solid frameworks

Paste this while your quiz or feedback form is still a draft.

---

I am designing [your teaching tool, such as a quiz or feedback form] for [your learners] so they can [the outcome you want]. If anything is unclear, ask me first.

Compare it with these four frameworks, one at a time. For each, say briefly where it fits and where it does not.

1. Miller's pyramid (knows, knows how, shows how, does): which level does it reach?
2. R2C2 (relationship, reactions, content, coaching): which steps of feedback does it support?
3. Six Principles of Learning Design (6PoLD, Bound and Chia): list the six, then say which it honours and which it ignores.
4. Zone of Proximal Development: what can learners do with it that they could not yet do alone, and when should the help fade?

Name each original source, not a later summary. I will check them. If you do not know a framework, say so. Mark anything you cannot source "to check".

Finish with the single change that would move it up one level on Miller's pyramid.
