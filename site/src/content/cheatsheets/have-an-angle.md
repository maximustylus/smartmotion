# Have an angle

Paste this before you ask for another version.

---

I am making: [what it is, in one line].
It is for: [who will read, watch or use it].
My angle, the one thing I want them to take away: [one sentence].

Before you make anything, read my angle. If it is too vague to guide your choices, do not guess. Say so, ask me one question that would sharpen it, and wait for my answer.

If my angle is clear, please do all four steps in one reply, in this order:
1. Say my angle back to me in your own words.
2. Tell me what you would leave out to protect it.
3. Make [choose one: an outline, a first draft, or a rewrite of the draft in this chat].
4. Show me up to three places where you may have drifted from my angle, so I can check them. If you see none, say so.

I will decide what stays.
