Here is what I am making: [one line]. Here is who it is for: [audience]. Here is my angle, the one thing I want them to take away: [angle].

Before you produce anything, restate my angle in your own words and tell me what you would cut to protect it. Then produce [the thing], and after it list three places where you drifted from the angle, if any. If my angle is too vague to protect, say so and ask me one question that would sharpen it.
