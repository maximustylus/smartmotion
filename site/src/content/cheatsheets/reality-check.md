List everything between this demo and something I could hand to a stranger, grouped as: correctness, failure handling, data and privacy, accessibility, performance on a weak phone, and maintenance.

For each group estimate the work as a share of what has been done so far, and name the one item most likely to take longest. Be blunt. If the honest answer is that the last ten percent is most of the work, say that first.
