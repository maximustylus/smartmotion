# Know the hook, keep the engagement

Paste this when planning a session, a lesson, or a talk.

---

I am running [a session] for [audience] lasting [minutes]. Propose an opening that the audience does rather than watches, taking under [n] minutes, needing only what they already have in their hands. Then propose one moment every [10] minutes that brings their own input back on screen.

For each proposal give: what the audience does, what I show, what could go wrong, and the fallback if the technology fails. Keep any data collected anonymous and aggregate only. Flag anything that depends on a service being reachable.
